Colophon. The method of diagramming is that of Alonzo Reed and Brainerd Kellogg, whose Higher Lessons in English (1877) taught a century of schoolchildren to draw a sentence. The words are set in the Fell Types, cut for Bishop John Fell of Oxford in the seventeenth century and revived by Igino Marini.

The apparatus parses English by rule rather than by understanding, and it will sometimes err. Where it does, open the Parsing beneath a figure and correct the part of speech. The sentence travels in the page's address, so any figure can be shared.
